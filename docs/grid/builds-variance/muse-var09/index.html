HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Goal

Smash a tunnel through the wall, then park the ball ABOVE the bricks (TOP ZONE). Up there your multiplier ramps fast, effects intensify, and bricks melt on their own.

🕹 Controls

  • Mouse: move = paddle target · click / Space = launch
  • Keys: ←→↑↓ / WASD · Space launch · P/Esc pause · M mute
  • Paddle Y: paddle roams bottom ~18%. Flick UP ↑ into the ball = POWER HIT (faster). Drift DOWN ↓ = soft catch (slower).
  • Touch: Drag mode — drag anywhere, paddle follows finger offset above. Stick mode — hold still to plant a floating thumbstick, push to drive velocity. Toggle on title or auto.
  • Gamepad: left stick / d-pad moves, A / Start launches & pauses.
