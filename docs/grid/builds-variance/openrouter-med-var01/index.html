Break through. Get on top. Go exponential.

How to play

  • Move: mouse, touch drag, or thumbstick (bottom-left). Paddle moves up & down too!
  • PUNCH: move up at the moment of contact to blast the ball faster — the key to Wedge & Kinetic bricks and getting Over the Top.
  • Wedge bricks need steep hits (>55°). Kinetic need fast balls. Phase blink — hit while solid. Volatile explode. Gold = big points.
  • Ride your ball above the bricks: the multiplier doubles every 2 seconds, up to x64.
  • Keys: WASD/arrows move · Space launch · P/Esc pause · M mute.
