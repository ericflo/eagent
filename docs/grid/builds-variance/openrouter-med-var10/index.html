BREAKTHROUGH

Break through the field. Get above it. Go frenzied.

MOUSE · TOUCH · WASD / ARROWS

HOW TO PLAY

MOVE

Mouse or drag anywhere on screen — the paddle follows both axes.

Thumbstick: tap the STICK toggle in game for a floating stick.

Move UP when striking to launch the ball harder.

SPECIAL BRICKS

◐ Angle-locked: only breaks on steep hits (|vy| dominant).

≋ Speed-gated: needs a fast ball. Fast balls glow white.

↔ Moving: slides side to side. Time it.

◌ Phasing: solid only when it shimmers solid. Watch its rhythm.

✸ Bomb: destroys neighbours when broken.

↻ Regenerating: grows back unless you clear fast.

BALLS & DROPS

Fireball burns through anything. Heavy smashes gates. Splitter splits on every hit. Bouncy multiplies score.

Drops: Multi ×3 balls, Sticky catch & aim, Wide paddle, Lasers, Slow-mo, plus ball-type capsules.

GET ON TOP: any ball above the brick field = FRENZY. Multiplier climbs the longer you stay up.
