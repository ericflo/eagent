BREAKTHROUGH

a modern Breakout about getting on top of the wall

HOW TO PLAY

  • 2D PADDLE: move left/right AND up/down. Moving up while striking the ball = SMASH (extra speed + whoosh). Side motion adds angle & english.
  • BREAKTHROUGH: open a hole and send the ball above the brick wall. Up there the ball bounces on its own while a RUSH multiplier compounds — the screen goes wild, the music escalates. Fall below the wall and the rush ends.
  • SMART BRICKS: no hit-point slogs — bricks are tricky by angle, speed, position or special balls: shielded (break from the open side), phase (need ball speed), deflectors, movers, bombs, armored cores.
  • CONTROLS: Mouse move + click · Touch: drag anywhere = virtual thumbstick, tap = launch · Keyboard: WASD/arrows + Space. P pauses, M mutes.
