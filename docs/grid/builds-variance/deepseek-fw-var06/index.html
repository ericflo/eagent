ROOFTOP
BREAKOUT

Get the ball on the roof of the bricks. Then hold on.

Drag with the mouse or use the thumbstick to move the paddle. Move up when you hit the ball to launch it faster. Break bricks to score; get above them to take the rooftop — that's where the real fun starts.

Standard Breaks on any hit
Crown Sits on the roof. Clearing crowns grows the Rooftop bonus
Angle Breaks only at a steep angle
Speed Breaks only when hit fast
Gold Indestructible. Deflects and speeds up the ball
Bomb Explodes, blasting nearby bricks
Reborn Breaks only if hit again quickly, else it regrows
Top Breaks only when hit from above — get on the roof!
Wild Always drops a power-up
