HOW TO PLAY

The goal

Break through the brick wall so the ball gets above it. While the ball is up there hitting bricks you are in FRENZY: the multiplier ramps ×1 → ×8, the screen escalates, and everything scores more. When the ball drops back down, the multiplier decays.

Move & smash

The paddle moves up and down too (bottom band). Moving up at contact SMASHES the ball faster; moving down absorbs speed for control. Where you hit the paddle bends the angle.
