HOW TO PLAY

Goal: clear all breakable bricks. Punch a hole through the barrier, then get ball(s) ABOVE the glowing ROOFTOP line — up there the multiplier doubles (x2→x4→x8…x99), score ticks on its own, and the whole arena goes wild.

  • Move: mouse follows · drag anywhere on touch (finger offset so you can see) · ←→↑↓ / WASD. Paddle roams left/right + up/down in its track.
  • Smash: hit the ball while flicking UP to add speed + shockwave. Faster ball = more score.
  • Launch: Space / tap LAUNCH. You have 3 lives + shield saves.

BRICK LEGEND

GLASS — breaks on anything.
VOLT ⚡ — needs SPEED (glows when a ball is fast enough). Too slow = bounce + "TOO SLOW".
APEX ⌃ — needs STEEP hit (within ~40° of head-on). Flat hits bounce off.
SKIM ≋ — needs SHALLOW graze. Head-on hits bounce off.
SPECTRAL 👻 — only EMBER fire or SPECTRE ghost balls can break it.
SHIFTER — slides side to side (otherwise like glass).
