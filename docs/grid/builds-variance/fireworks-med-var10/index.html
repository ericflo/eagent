SKYBREAK

Break through the canopy. Get on top. Go wild.

How to play

  • Move the paddle with mouse, finger, or WASD / arrows. It moves up and down — hit the ball while moving up for a smash.
  • The heart of the game: break a hole in the canopy and get the ball on top of the bricks. Your multiplier ramps the whole time you stay up there.
  • Special bricks need the right angle, enough speed, a heavy/fire ball — or a bomb next door. Never just “more hits”.
  • Catch capsules: Fire, Multi, Heavy, Ghost, Expand, Sticky, Laser, Shield, Life.
  • Launch the ball with tap / click / space. P pauses, M mutes.
