HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Goal

Clear every breakable brick. You have 3 lives. Tunnel a ball above the bricks to trigger ★ OVER THE TOP ★ — multiplier climbs while you're up there, then BANK it on the way down.

🕹️ Controls

  • Mouse: paddle follows cursor · click to launch / fire lasers
  • Keyboard: AD/←→ move · WS/↑↓ rise & dive · Space launch / pause in menus
  • Touch: drag near the paddle = 1:1 direct · drag anywhere else = thumbstick · tap = launch / fire
  • Smash: move the paddle UP fast as the ball lands = SMASH! bonus speed + score.
  • P/Esc pause · M mute

🧱 Bricks (no HP — all 1 hit, but picky)
