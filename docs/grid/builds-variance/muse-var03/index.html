HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Goal

Smash every brick. Punch a hole, get the ball ABOVE the brick field to trigger OVERDRIVE — multiplier ramps exponentially while it bounces up top!

🏓 Paddle 2-D

Move left/right across the full width and up/down in the zone near the bottom. Hit the ball while flicking UP ↑ to launch it faster & steeper + bonus points.

🧱 Tricky bricks

No boring HP bricks. Watch the telegraphs:
⇕ GATE steep angle only   ≫ SPEED fast ball only
◌ GHOST solid only   ↔ MOVER slides   ▼ SHIELD one side only

✨ Power-ups

M multiball F fire pierce L lightning B big S sticky Z laser (Space/tap) T slow-mo W wide +1 life

🎮 Controls

Mouse: move = paddle (x+y), click/Space = launch & laser.
Keys: ←→↑↓ / WASD move, Space launch, P pause.
Touch: drag left-half = thumbstick (relative), drag near paddle = direct grab.
