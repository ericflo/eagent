HOW TO PLAY

MOVE — mouse / finger drags the paddle anywhere in the lower zone. Keys: ←→↑↓ or WASD. Release the ball: click / tap / SPACE.

BOOST HIT — hit the ball while your paddle is moving upward for explosive speed. Fast balls smash SPEED bricks (⚡) and fly through tunnels.

ANGLE bricks (↑) break only when struck from the direction of the arrow. Wrong side = clang.

COMBO — every brick without touching the paddle raises the multiplier. Paddle touch or a lost ball resets it.

▼ ▲ ◀ ▶ angle bricks · ⚡ speed · ● bomb

OVERDRIVE — get a ball above the top brick row: rainbow mayhem, the combo ramps on its own, and the score explodes.

PICKUPS — catch falling capsules: COMET (fast, glowing, extra points), HEAVY (smashes straight through), SPLIT (multiball), GROW (bigger paddle), ♥ (extra life). Bonus balls never cost a life.

PAUSE — P or ESC. MUTE — M.
