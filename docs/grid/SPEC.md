# A Modern Take on Breakout

I think it would be fun to build a new, modern take on a Breakout-style game.

I have strong memories of playing Brickles in the school computer lab whenever I had free time. When I wasn't playing Pizza to Go or Oregon Trail, I was playing Brickles. My favorite part was when you managed to break the bricks in just the right way that the ball got up above them and just bounced around on top on its own.

## Core concept

I'd like to make a version of Breakout that's all about that moment: setting up situations where you have to break your way through to the "fun part," where the ball gets on top of the bricks and keeps going by itself. Once you get the ball up there, things should get exponentially more awesome.

## Game feel and feedback

In a modern Breakout game, it's really important to have a lot of "game juice" — lots of visual effects. As your score and multiplier go up and you're doing better and better, more visual and audible things should happen to signify that you're powered up.

## Balls and bricks

A single ball breaking through standard bricks probably isn't fun enough. There should be fun special power-up balls and special difficult bricks. I hate bricks that just have to be hit multiple times, so let's come up with bricks that are difficult in other ways — for example, bricks you have to hit at a certain angle, or at a certain speed.

## Paddle movement

Instead of only moving left and right, it would be cool if the paddle could also move up and down a little. If you're moving up when you hit the ball, it gives the ball a bit more acceleration. That would add a whole new dimension to the game — plus lots of special effects, power-ups, and so on.

## Controls

It should be playable with a mouse, but we should assume a lot of people will be playing this on their mobile phones, so thumb/thumbstick controls should be a first-class citizen.
